Ratatouille

Description

Layered ratatouille, also known as confit byaldi, is a visually stunning French Provençal dish featuring thinly sliced vegetables like eggplant, zucchini, yellow squash, and tomatoes arranged in alternating concentric circles or rows over a bed of flavorful tomato sauce infused with herbs, garlic, and onions. The assembly is drizzled with olive oil and baked until the vegetables are tender and caramelized, creating a harmonious blend of flavors and textures that's both elegant and rustic. Often served as a side or main vegetarian course, it's popularized by the Pixar film "Ratatouille" and emphasizes fresh summer produce for a light yet satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Eggplant: One eggplant adds a nice purple colour to the dish.
  • Zucchini: Add two of these and slice them up to add more colour.
  • Roma tomatoes: Slice these up to add red to the arrangement.
  • Crushed tomatoes: This will be used for the sauce.
  • Carrot: Shredded, added to the sauce.
  • Garlic cloves: Important for adding flavour.
  • Onion: To bring out more flavour and armomatics.
  • Olive oil: A signature fat needed to saute the onions and garlic.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190C/375F.
  2. In a large nonstick pan, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium-high heat.
  3. Add in the onion, garlic and carrot, and cook until tender.
  4. Add in the crushed tomatoes and seasonings and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper.
  6. Once the sauce has thickened, pour into a 2 quart baking dish.
  7. Arrange the sliced eggplant, zucchini and tomatoes in a circular pattern standing upright within the dish containing the sauce.
  8. Brush the sliced vegetables with remaining olive oil.
  9. Cover and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake until vegetables are tender.
  10. Serve the dish immediately.
