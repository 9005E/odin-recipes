Bean Burrito

Description

The Mexican bean burrito is a popular Tex-Mex dish consisting of a soft flour tortilla wrapped around a filling of refried beans, typically made from pinto or black beans seasoned with spices like cumin, garlic, and chili for a savory flavor. It often includes additional ingredients such as shredded cheese, rice, diced onions, tomatoes, lettuce, and salsa to enhance texture and taste, making it a hearty, portable meal. This versatile vegetarian option can be grilled or steamed for extra crispiness and is commonly served with sides like guacamole or sour cream.

Ingredients

  • Black beans: 800g rinsed and drained. Can optionally use pinto beans.
  • Olive oil: 1 tbsp.
  • Red onion: 1/2, finely diced.
  • Garlic: 1 tsp, freshly minced.
  • Red capsicum (bell pepper): Use 1, finely diced.
  • Sweet paprika: 2 tsp.
  • Ground cumin: 1 tsp.
  • Oregano: 1 tsp, dried.
  • Garlic powder: 1 tsp.
  • Sea salt flakes: 1 tsp, or to taste.
  • Black pepper: 1/2 tsp, cracked, to taste.
  • Tomato paste: 2 tbsp, concentrated puree.

For the salsa

  • Tomato: 1, finely diced.
  • Coriander (cilantro): 1/4 bunch, finely chopped.
  • Red onion: The second 1/2, finely diced.
  • Lime juice: 1 tbsp.
  • Sea salt flakes: 1/2 tsp, or to taste.

For assembly

  • Flour tortillas: 4 large.
  • Cooked rice: 2 cups, optionally 1 x 250g packet microwave rice.
  • Melting cheese: 1 cup, shredded (optional).

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add onion, garlic and capsicum and cook while stirring for 1-2 minutes until softened.
  3. Add in the black beans, paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
  4. Stir the tomato paste and add 1/4 cup water. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 4-6 minutes until sauce is thickend, then set aside to cool slightly.
  5. Add the salsa ingredients to a medium bowl, and toss with spoon until well combined.
  6. Place the flour tortillas on a flat surface and place 1/2 cup rice just below the centre.
  7. Optionally, top with 1/4 of the shredded cheese, then top with 1/4 of the bean mixture and salsa.
  8. Fold up the bottom of the tortilla high enough to cover the filling, then fold the sides in and roll up tightly.
  9. Preheat a large pan over medium heat and add the burritos to the pan, seam side down.
  10. Cook the burritos for 1-2 minutes until golden, then flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden on other side.
  11. Serve the burritos while hot, optionally with avocado dip or sour cream.
